Privacy Policy

Pantler · effective 8 November 2026

Pantler is an app for knowing what's in your kitchen. This page says what it stores, where, who can see it, and what you can do about it. It is written from the app as built, not from a template. Where the app changes, this page changes, and the date above moves.

The short version

  • Pantler stores an email address, a first name, and a list of food, including where it was bought and what it cost. If you take them, it also stores photographs of that food.
  • In the app, your kitchen is visible only to the people in your household. We look at it only to run the service, or when you send us a receipt.
  • There is no advertising and no tracking. The only analytics are a weekly count of how many kitchens are being kept up, and the diagnostics Google's ML Kit collects on Android, both described below.
  • Nothing is sold, or shared for advertising or marketing. Every service that handles data for us is named below.
  • You can export your kitchen or delete your account from inside the app, and you can ask for deletion without the app.

What stays on your phone

Reading the text off a receipt, and reading a barcode, happen on the phone. Reminders are scheduled on the phone; there is no push-notification service and no device token is collected. A copy of your shopping list is kept on the phone so it works in a shop with no signal.

Who can see your data

In the app, the people in your household, on their own phones. Every request to the database is checked against your household membership on the server, so one household can never read another's kitchen.

We, the people who make Pantler, can access the database to run the service and to fix problems, and we look at households' data for no other reason. Once a week we count how many kitchens are being kept up. For each kitchen, numbered rather than named, that count shows how many people are in it, how long it has existed, how many things went in and out that week and overall, and how many have sat untouched for three weeks. It shows no item names and nobody's name.

Where it's stored

In the United States. If you use Pantler from the European Union or the United Kingdom, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States under Supabase's standard contractual clauses. You have the rights described below wherever you are.

How long we keep it

  • Your food and its history stay for as long as your household exists. Things you've used or thrown out are kept on purpose: they're what "how much did we waste this month" is counted from. You can see recent ones in Insights and correct them at any time.
  • Photos stay with your household's items and their history, and are deleted when the household is deleted.
  • A receipt you sent us, and its photo, are deleted within 90 days of being sent.
  • Your account exists until you delete it.
  • Backups: the database is backed up daily and each backup is kept for 7 days, so something deleted can remain in a backup for up to a week before it disappears.
  • Request logs, which include your phone's IP address, are kept by our database host for 7 days.

What you can do

  • Export your kitchen. Household, then Your data, then Export my data. A file of your household's items and their history, shareable from the phone.
  • Leave a household. The kitchen stays with the others, and they stop seeing your name on what you added.
  • Delete your account. Household, then Your data, then Delete my account. Your profile and sign-in are removed at once. If you were the last person in the household, the household, everything in it, its history and its photos are deleted with it. Reminders already scheduled on your phone may still appear until you remove the app. If you're not in a household, or can't use the app, ask us to delete your account.
  • Correct anything. Every name, date, photo and note can be changed by anyone in the household. Whatever you did last is what the app believes.
  • Ask us. For anything else, such as a copy of your data in another form or a question about this page, write to pantler.help@gmail.com.

If you're in the UK or the EU

We use your data to provide the app you signed up for. We use a receipt you send us because you chose to send it, and you can withdraw that by asking us to delete it. We use your data for security, backups and the weekly count because we have a legitimate interest in running a reliable service. You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to or restrict how we use it, by writing to the address above. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission.

Children

Everyone in a household signs in with their own account, so accounts are for people 13 and over. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we'll delete it. A household can of course keep food for children who don't have accounts.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are hashed. Access is checked on the server for every request. Photos are private to the household and served through links that expire within a day. No system is perfect; if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you.

Changes

When the app starts storing something new, this page says so before it does, and the date at the top changes.

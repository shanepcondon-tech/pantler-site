Pantler

The Pantry App.

Know what's in your kitchen, what's about to go, and what to buy. Shared with whoever shares your fridge.

Put things in as they arrive — a photo, a name, done. Pantler keeps track, reminds you in time, builds the shopping list from what you're running out of, matches the receipt to what you bought, and tells you what you could cook tonight with what's about to go. It's built for two people and one fridge, and for people who can't see the shelf.

What it stands for

It's built never to claim what it doesn't know. A date is read from the packet, chosen by you, or marked as an estimate.

Blind users are the design, not an add-on. Every card is one spoken sentence — the name, the state, where it is on the shelf. Colour never carries a meaning alone.

Your data stays home. Your kitchen is for your household. Nothing is sold, and there's no advertising or tracking. The whole policy fits on one page.

Your answer wins. Over the barcode database, the shop's receipt, the app's memory. Corrections are one tap and always allowed.

Numbers come with their receipts. "About" means about, figures say what they're based on, and nobody in a kitchen is ranked.

Get it

Pantler is in testing on iPhone and Android. Ask for an invitation and we'll send you one.

Pantler is made by Shane Condon in Virginia. The name is a medieval word for the person who kept a household's bread and provisions.

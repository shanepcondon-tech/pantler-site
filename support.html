We read everything. Say which phone you're on and, if it's about one thing in your kitchen, what it's called.

Before you write

  • How Pantler works is inside the app: Household, then How Pantler works. Most questions are answered there, including what the colours mean, where the dates come from, and how the shopping list fills itself.
  • The app updates itself. Open it twice, or tap the banner when it says a newer version is ready. The version you're running is under Help on the Household screen; include it when you write.
  • A receipt that read wrongly can be sent from inside the app, from the "What did it read?" screen. The people who make Pantler use it to teach the reader that shop's layout, and the report and its photo are deleted within 90 days. The privacy page says exactly what happens to it.

Your data

Export your kitchen's items and their history from Household, then Your account, then Export my data. What we store and why is on the privacy page.

Delete your Pantler account

This is for Pantler: The Pantry App, by Shane Condon. You can delete your account in the app, or ask us to delete it for you without the app.

In the app

Household, then Your account, then Delete my account. The app says what would be deleted; choose Continue, then type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted at once. Not in a household? The newest version of the app has Delete my account on the Household screen too; on an older version, email us instead.

Without the app

Email pantler.help@gmail.com from the email address you sign in to Pantler with, and say "Delete my Pantler account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you can no longer use that address, say so and we'll find another way to check it's you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password) and your profile (your first name and your settings).
  • If you were the last person in your household: the household, everything in its kitchen, its history, its shopping list, its recipes and its photos (any photos the app can't remove at that moment go in our weekly clean-up).
  • A receipt you sent us, and its photo, are deleted within 90 days of being sent, whether or not you delete your account.

What stays, and for how long

  • If other people are still in your household, the kitchen stays with them, including things you added. Your name is removed from them.
  • Copies in the daily database backups disappear within 7 days, and request logs within 7 days.
  • The text of a receipt you sent may be kept indefinitely as a test example for the receipt reader, with card, loyalty, customer and approval numbers and people's names removed, and without who sent it.
  • Reminders already scheduled on your phone may still appear until you remove the app.

Accessibility

Pantler is built for VoiceOver and TalkBack first. If a screen doesn't read well, that's a bug we want to hear about. Say which screen and what it said.
